How Driver Onboarding Works

  1. Create Your Account: Sign in with your registered Go Ride customer account.
  2. Review Requirements: Hold an active commercial or private transport vehicle license.
  3. In-Person Fleet Verification: Document verification (Driving License, Vehicle RC, and Identity) is completed in-person with Go Ride fleet officers to guarantee data safety and identity privacy.
🛡️ Privacy Protection Notice: Go Ride never collects national identity numbers (such as Aadhaar or raw license numbers) on public web forms. Official KYC verification is conducted securely during direct onboarding.
